Rayya Subandar

Head of Fashion and Furniture

We Bridge Markets, Elevating Conscious Creation

Across Indonesian Sea's Fashion & Furniture expansion sector is headquartered in London. We manage trade, logistics, and partnerships to bring you the best of Indonesian craftsmanship. Specializing in sustainable exports, we connect buyers with timeless, high-quality clothing and furniture directly from Indonesian producers.

Our partners offer a diverse range of eco-conscious materials including vegan leather made of coffee waste, handwoven fabrics, naturally dyed organic cotton, and upcycled textiles. Buyers can purchase raw fabrics and materials, or choose from ready-to-wear designs by our partners. Every piece is traceable—from cotton growth and coffee waste collection, to the moment it's carefully packed and shipped across oceans. Material samples are available upon request.

OUR PRODUCERS' MATERIALS

From raw materials, to products with purpose

M-Tex Leather

M-Tex is a leather-like material made from fermented coffee skin waste. M-Tex is a solid, durable sheet material with a smooth surface that can be shaped and finished for different design needs.

Bell Living LabWest Java

WHAT IT CAN BE


Made from: Coffee skin waste through bacterial fermentation
Applications: Furniture surfaces, interior panels, fashion accessories, and customisable product design
Key features: Plant-based, biodegradable, and a unique alternative to leather with a modern, sustainable touch
Strength: 13,25 MPa
Color available: Black, Brown, Red
Certification: PETA Vegan Approved, USDA Biobased Product


Standard sheets: 90 × 90 cm
Maximum: up to 6 meters long

C-Foam

C-Foam is a cork-like material made from coffee husk and spent coffee grounds. With its warm, natural texture, C-Foam not only looks organic but also functions as an effective sound absorber. It has a sound absorption coefficient of 0.4–0.7, performing on par with conventional acoustic foam panels—making it a smart, sustainable option for interior applications.
Bell Living Lab West Java

WHAT IT CAN BE


Made from: Coffee husk and spent coffee grounds
Sound absorption: Coefficient 0.4 – 0.7 (similar to acoustic foam panels)
Applications: Acoustic wall panels, interior elements, design accents, and furniture inserts
Key features: Customizable shapes, organic texture, eco-friendly, combines beauty with function

The forming process enables precise edge definition, clean molds, and unique profiles that meet your design or functional requirements. This opens new possibilities for designers, brands, and architects to craft their own material stories.

Kalpa

Kalpa is a board-like material made from agricultural waste, combining coffee husk and coffee grounds into a strong yet natural-looking surface. Each sheet features a warm, earthy tone with a grainy texture, bringing an organic character that adds depth and comfort to modern spaces.
Bell Living Lab West Java

WHAT IT CAN BE

Made from: Coffee husk, coffee grounds, and other upcycled plant-based fibers
Applications: Wall panels, tiles, decorative surfaces, furniture components, and creative packaging
Key features: Natural earthy aesthetics, modular and pattern-combinable, adaptable shapes
