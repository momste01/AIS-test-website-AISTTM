What We Ship

Traceable goods. One source.

From West Java's coffee highlands to the coconut belt and the workshops of the archipelago, we source and export Indonesian goods directly to buyers across North America and Europe. Pick the line you're here for.

Why AIS

Built on Indonesian roots

01

Deep origin knowledge

We've built lasting relationships with producers across Indonesia, sourcing exceptional goods that remain largely undiscovered by the global market.

02

Traceable, verified, and accountable

We know the name behind every shipment. No middlemen, no ambiguity. Complete documentation from origin to your door, quality checked at both ends. If it doesn't meet our standards, it doesn't ship.

03

Built on relationships

You are never a number. Every trade is personal, with responsive support and a genuine commitment to long-term partnership. Our mission is long-term value, not just volume.
